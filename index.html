What it does

Four things, done properly.

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs. Just the payment channel, and a record you control.

Works with zero data

The *99# USSD service and 123PAY IVR run on the ordinary phone signal. That is what PaySetu uses, so a dead data connection is not a dead end.

USSDIVROffline

No internet permission

The app declares no INTERNET permission. Android will not let it open a connection, so there is no code path that could leak anything.

Encrypted history

Your payment records live in a SQLCipher database whose key is wrapped by the Android Keystore. The key never leaves the device.

Your PIN stays yours

PaySetu never asks for, sees or stores your UPI PIN. You type it into your bank's own session, which the app cannot read.

Confirmation, not guesswork

The bank's confirmation SMS is read on the phone and turned into a short summary. The message text itself is never stored.

Step by step

How a payment works.

Four steps, and the app steps out of the way for the third one — the one that matters.

1

Scan a QR, or paste a UPI ID

Point the camera at any UPI QR code, or type the UPI ID. The code is decoded on the phone; no image is saved or sent.

2

PaySetu opens the right channel

Your dialer opens on the *99# session (or dials the 123PAY number), with the payee's UPI ID already on the clipboard, ready to paste.

3

You complete it with your bank

The payment happens inside your bank's own session. You choose the amount and enter your UPI PIN there — PaySetu is not part of it and cannot read it.

4

The bank's SMS confirms it

PaySetu reads your bank's confirmation SMS, shows you the outcome, and keeps a summary in your encrypted history.

Straight answers

What PaySetu is not.

Worth saying plainly, because the category is full of apps that are all of these.

Not a bank

It holds no money and has no account of yours. Your balance lives with your bank.

Not a payment aggregator

It does not route, process or settle payments, and it never touches UPI servers.

Not a wallet

Nothing is stored for spending later. Each payment goes straight to your bank.

Not affiliated

Independent of NPCI and of every bank. It simply prepares their official channel.
