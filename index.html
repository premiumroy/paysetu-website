Pay over UPI, even without mobile data

PaySetu is an Android app for the two UPI channels that work on a plain phone signal: the NPCI *99# USSD service and 123PAY. Scan a QR code, PaySetu opens the right session for you, and your bank's confirmation SMS tells the app the result.

Works offline

No mobile data or Wi-Fi needed. The payment travels over the *99# USSD channel or the 123PAY call.

No internet permission

The app cannot open a network connection, so nothing about you can leave your phone.

Encrypted history

Your payment records stay on the device, encrypted with SQLCipher and a key held by the Android Keystore.

Your PIN stays yours

PaySetu never sees, asks for, or stores your UPI PIN. You enter it in your bank's own session.

How a payment works

  • You scan a UPI QR code, or paste a UPI ID, and confirm the amount.
  • PaySetu opens your dialer on the *99# session (or dials the 123PAY number) with the payee's UPI ID copied to your clipboard, ready to paste.
  • You complete the payment with your bank and enter your UPI PIN there.
  • Your bank sends a confirmation SMS. PaySetu reads it and shows you whether the payment went through, and keeps a record.

What PaySetu is not

PaySetu is not a bank, a payment aggregator, or a wallet. It does not hold your money, it cannot move money on its own, and it is not affiliated with NPCI or with any bank. It simply prepares the official channel and reports what your bank tells you. The payment itself is between you and your bank.
