Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Roy Limited ("Roy Limited", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the PaySetu mobile application for Android (the "Application").

It applies to the Application and to nothing else. It does not apply to your bank, to the National Payments Corporation of India, to your mobile network operator, or to any other application on your device. Those parties handle your information under their own policies, and we have no control over them.

By installing and using the Application you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the Application.

Definitions

"Personal data" has the meaning given to it in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") — any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.

"Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, retrieval, use and erasure.

"You" means the individual using the Application, and "Device" means the Android device on which it is installed.

Our role

For the purposes of the DPDP Act, Roy Limited acts as the Data Fiduciary in respect of any personal data processed through the Application.

We state that role precisely because it has consequences. As a Data Fiduciary we are responsible for the lawful handling of personal data, for responding to your requests, and for the security of what we hold.

The design principle behind this Policy

The Application was built so that there is almost nothing for this Policy to describe.

PaySetu does not request the INTERNET permission. Android enforces this at the operating system level: an application without that permission cannot open a network socket, and therefore cannot transmit data off the device — by accident, by defect, or by any other means. There is no account, no registration, no subscription and no server that holds your information.

This is a deliberate architectural decision rather than a policy commitment. A commitment can be broken; a missing permission cannot.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the Application does not transmit to us or to any third party:

  1. Your name, email address, postal address, telephone number, or date of birth.
  2. Your contacts, call logs, calendar, photographs, files or media.
  3. Your precise or approximate location. The Application does not request location permission.
  4. Your advertising identifier, IMEI, device serial number, or any device identifier used to recognise you across applications.
  5. Usage analytics, behavioural telemetry, session recordings, or interaction logs.
  6. Crash or diagnostic reports transmitted off the device.
  7. Any biometric data.

We do not operate advertising in the Application, and the Application does not integrate any advertising, analytics or attribution software development kit. We do not sell, rent, license or otherwise disclose personal data to any third party, because we do not hold any.

Information processed on your device

The Application processes certain information locally, on your Device, in order to function. This information remains under your control at all times.

  • Payment records. When you complete a payment, the Application records the amount, the status, the payee identifier and the time, so that you have a history of your transactions.
  • Saved payees. Identifiers you choose to save for future payments.
  • Application preferences. Your selected appearance, your bank's name, notes you may add about your bank's menu, and your language and accessibility choices.
  • Clipboard. Where you ask the Application to assist with a payment, it may place a payee identifier on the system clipboard so that you need not re-enter it manually. The Application clears the clipboard once the payment process concludes.

None of the above is transmitted to us. It is not accessible to us. We cannot retrieve it, and we could not disclose it if asked.

Legal basis for processing

Where the DPDP Act requires a basis for processing, we rely on your consent, given when you grant a permission or enable a feature, and on the legitimate use of processing data for the purpose for which you provided it.

Consent is specific, informed, and may be withdrawn at any time by revoking the relevant permission or by uninstalling the Application. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

Storage and security

Information processed by the Application is stored in the Application's private storage area on your Device, which the Android operating system prevents other applications from reading.

Payment records and saved payees are held in encrypted form. The encryption key is generated on your Device and is protected by the Device's own secure hardware-backed storage; it is not written to any location that can be read by another application.

The backup facility, where you choose to use it, produces a file protected by a passphrase that you select. We do not receive, store or have any means of recovering that passphrase. A backup file that cannot be opened without your passphrase is the intended behaviour, not a limitation.

No method of storage is entirely without risk. We describe our arrangements in general terms because a detailed public description of a security implementation is itself a security weakness. We would rather be brief here than precise.

Retention

Information processed by the Application is retained on your Device for as long as you choose to retain it.

  • Individual payment records may be deleted from the history within the Application.
  • All information is removed when you uninstall the Application or clear its storage through your Device settings.
  • A backup file that you have exported is retained wherever you have stored it, under your control, and is unaffected by uninstalling the Application.

We hold no copy, and therefore have no retention period to disclose.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not share personal data with any third party. We cannot, because we do not hold it.

The Application interacts with your device's dialler and with your bank's UPI service in order to place the call and read the confirmation. Those interactions are between you and your bank. Roy Limited is not a party to them and receives no information from them.

We may disclose information if compelled by a lawful order. In practice there is no information in our possession that such an order could reach.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  1. Access — to obtain confirmation of whether we process your personal data, and a summary of it.
  2. Correction — to have inaccurate or incomplete personal data corrected.
  3. Erasure — to have your personal data erased, subject to applicable law.
  4. Grievance redressal — to have a complaint addressed, and to escalate it if you are not satisfied with the outcome.
  5. Nomination — to nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

Because the Application does not transmit personal data to us, in practice the answer to a request for access is that we hold nothing. Your information remains on your Device and under your direct control, and can be reviewed, corrected or deleted there.

Children

The Application is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. It is a payment instrument, and it has no mechanism to verify the age of its user.

We do not knowingly process personal data of children. If you believe a child has used the Application, please contact us using the details in the section below.

International transfers

We do not transfer personal data outside India, because we do not transfer personal data at all. All processing occurs on your Device, within your jurisdiction.
